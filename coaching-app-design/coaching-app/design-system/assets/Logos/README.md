# Logos

Rising chevrons over a dumbbell, redrawn as flat SVG from the owner's Gemini exploration. Single-ink files: `coaching-app-mark.svg` and `coaching-app-lockup.svg` are ink `#111410` (use on `surface`, `surface-raised`, `lime`); `coaching-app-mark-lime.svg` is lime `#c6f432` and `coaching-app-lockup-inverse.svg` is lime + `#f4f5f0` (use on dark `surface` or `contrast`). `coaching-app-icon.svg` carries its own lime square. The lockup text is outlined Barlow Condensed 800.
